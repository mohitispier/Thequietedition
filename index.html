1. How online designer fashion has changed

Thanks to the internet, finding and buying designer clothing and accessories has become a lot easier. Previously, you would have had to go into a range of stores, including department stores, boutiques and individual stores, to see their luxurious ranges. There are now many more options available from online stores, and you can easily access products from a variety of markets.

There are more choices available to you. You could spend hours looking at different materials, sizes, price points and details of each product. There are hundreds of options for individual items like coats, handbags and shoes from various websites.

A useful rule: An organised online designer store will be easy to navigate. The website will have clear categories, designers, detailed information and helpful search tools — all of which help you find the right products.

5. What to check before buying from an online designer store

Do some research when purchasing from online sites. A few things are worth checking first:

Read the product description

Make sure the product has all the essential information, such as materials, sizes and colours. Don’t just rely on the images.

Research the retailer’s policies

How will the item be delivered, how long will it take to arrive, and will there be duty or tax charges? What can you return, how long do you have to return it, and will there be any charges?

Look into their customer service

Who can you email? There should be easy ways to see who to contact, including a ‘Contact us’ page. How easy is it to get help with your order?

Consider what you need

Even if it is a famous designer, it may not suit your wardrobe. The most useful item to purchase is one that matches your style, budget and needs.

A different way to discover luxury fashion
