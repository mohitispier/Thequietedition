The Row Handbags: A Look at Minimalist Luxury and the Margaux Collection

Clean lines, minimal details and no big logos — a look at The Row’s handbags, from the recognisable Margaux to totes and shoulder bags, and how to choose the right one for you.

Some bags have a distinctive look, whether they are colourful, covered in logos or very flashy. Others are much simpler in design, with cleaner lines, minimal details and an elegant feel. The Row handbags are a good example.

The Row was founded in 2006 by Mary-Kate and Ashley Olsen. Throughout the brand’s history, the focus has been on simple designs, thoughtful details and high-quality materials. This is reflected in its handbag range, which offers simple totes, structured top-handle bags and minimalist shoulder bags.

1. What makes The Row handbags special?

Many people instantly notice how minimal they look. There are no big logos or flashy details.

They are easy to match with different outfits. The simple leather designs can be worn with an office outfit, casual jeans, smart trousers or a dressier look, and the neutral colours make them easy to wear throughout the year.

Because the designs are simple, they don’t rely on fashion trends. Many of the styles have a classic look, which can appeal to anyone building a wardrobe of timeless pieces.

2. The Row Margaux

One of the best-known designs in the collection is the Margaux. It has a structured yet relaxed look, with top handles and subtle detailing on the sides. Its simple style makes it easy to recognise.

The Margaux has become strongly linked with the quiet luxury trend. It has a classic handbag look with a relaxed finish, so it works with everyday outfits while still looking stylish.

The Margaux has been available in different sizes, colours and materials. When choosing between styles, consider how much space you need and how you would like to carry your bag.

Choosing a size: if you carry lots of everyday items, a larger size may be best. If you don’t need much, you may prefer a smaller style.

3. Other The Row bag styles

Although the Margaux is a popular choice, there are other options.

Park ToteA great option if you are looking for something simple. Its sleek, relaxed design is perfect for carrying everyday items and works well with casual and simple work outfits.
Half MoonA curved design reminiscent of traditional shoulder bags. Less structured than the top-handle bags, it is a great option if you want a relaxed bag for simple outfits.
SofiaA structured, sleek design with elegant elements. It has a more formal feel than the relaxed tote and shoulder bags, so it pairs well with a smart outfit.

As you can see, there are many styles to choose from within minimalist fashion.

4. How to choose the right designer shoulder bag

When choosing your luxury handbag, think about how you plan to use it. Consider:

  • SizeIf you carry a lot, a large tote may be useful. If you only need your phone, wallet and keys, a smaller shoulder bag may be enough.
  • StructureStructured bags usually have a more formal look, whereas unstructured bags have a more relaxed appearance.
  • ColourBlack, brown and other neutral colours are easy to match. If your wardrobe is already full of neutrals, you could also go for a coloured bag.
  • MaterialThink about the detailing. Different leathers can have different textures.
  • ComfortCheck the length of the handles and straps. Even if a bag looks lovely, it won’t be very functional if it isn’t comfortable.

These little details can help you find the right bag for your lifestyle.

5. Should you consider minimalist luxury handbags?

Minimalist luxury handbags can be a great option if you like simple designs. Because they are minimal, they are often easier to match with different outfits. Their classic design also means they can stay useful even when fashion trends change.

However, a high price doesn’t automatically make a bag a good choice. Think about the design, material, build and functionality, and how often you would use the bag. It is also a good idea to weigh the cost of the bag against your budget.

The best bag for you is one that fits your personal style and can be used in your everyday life.

Looking at The Row handbags

The Row offers many minimalist luxury handbags, from the recognisable Margaux to simple totes and shoulder bags. Each bag has a different shape and purpose, so looking at a few different styles can help you decide.
